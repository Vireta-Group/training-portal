1. Executive Summary
TrainingPro is a multi-tenant, white-label, Bangla-English bilingual SaaS platform designed to
serve two major customer segments simultaneously:
1. Private vocational training centers, IT institutes, and language schools
2. Government, semi-government, and NGO skills development projects (SEIP, NSDA, NTPA, UCEP,
BRAC, PKSF, etc.)
The same software allows a private institute to run paid courses while also managing fully donor-
funded or subsidized training projects with 100% compliance reporting in one click.
